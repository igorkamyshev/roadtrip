# web/e2e: what the layout's specs check

What the screen's tests of the layout read, spec by spec ([spec #20](../../docs/specs/20.md) «Решения по тестам»). How the tests run, their doubles, time and shared helpers are in `../README.md`, «Tests».

**The layout's tests** (`e2e/layout.spec.ts`) read the panels' boxes (`boundingBox`) on 360×640, 390×844 and 430×932, and on what Safari leaves of the iPhone 15/16 (393×660) and SE (375×550):

- with the longest phrase, the panels apart, on the screen and 12 px from their edges in the normal and the pin mode, the carousel's current card among them, the data's sources as wide as the phrase, their place kept when they go;
- the current card leaving 64 px for the next one before the slider;
- with the longest and the shortest, the slider under the phrase and down to the bottom;
- with the shortest, an hour of the slider 31 and 22 px high on the two iPhones.

The wide screen's tests run on the windows of the amendment's table (spec #20 «Решения по тестам», #113), which `e2e/windows.ts` holds with the pointer and the layout of each: `WINDOWS`, `WIDE_WINDOWS`, the desktop `DESKTOP`, and `onWindow(window)` for `test.use`. Chromium without `hasTouch` answers `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, a mouse, so the touch rows set `hasTouch`; the phones of `layout.spec.ts` go without it, so they have the carousel's arrows too, which they do not read. The panels' readings `expectApart`, `overlap` and `fromEdges`, the open sheet's panel `sheetPanel` and where it stands `sheetPlace`, the city cards' locators `pointCards`, `pointCard` and `chosenCard`, the arrows' plate `carouselArrows` and the map's canvas `theMap` are in `e2e/screen.ts`. A later check of the wide screen joins these files on the same windows.

- `e2e/wide-layout.spec.ts`, the layout of each window:
  - The open «Гражданство» a modal in the middle on the wide ones, a sheet at the bottom on the narrow ones; the slider a row (horizontal to a screen reader) on the wide ones and a column on the narrow ones. A window crossing the threshold, 1440×900 → 390×844 → 1440×900, with «Гражданство» open, filtered, scrolled and a row focused: it stays open and changes its look, and its filter, scroll and focus, the chosen city and the slider's cell stay.
  - On the wide windows but the threshold's corner, whose row narrows for the arrows, in hours and days with the longest and the shortest phrase: the row 560 px long, its cells 40 px high, 12 px from the left and 8 px over the cards, the tab right of the hours and left of the days, and the phrase, the row and the cards wholly in view (`cardsInView`) apart and on the screen; the carousel the whole width, ending with «и ещё K» 12 px from the right, and not scrolling when its cards fit.
  - On every wide window the first card 12 px from the left and the bottom and as wide as with a ring in «Откуда едем?» (apart from the phrase, no slider), while calculating and on a failure (apart from the phrase and the slider's row); in the pin mode with the longest phrase the pin's panel apart from the phrase, 12 px from the left and the bottom, its buttons in the hint's line, or under it on 720×500, and where they fit one line with the same padding round it.
  - On every wide window, with the clock held and old visa rules, the phrase's plate (#103) with the longest and the shortest phrase: 12 px from the top and the left, no wider than 720 px and the window less 24, the date (`rulesDate`) and the first 5 s' sources in it, apart from the slider's row and the cards; after 5 s the sources gone, the plate lower with the same top, left and width, the row and the cards where they were; in the longest phrase each comma on the line of the word in brackets before it and «мои визы:» on one line, read from the text's boxes (`badBreaks`); and on 768×1024 the short phrase on one line.
  - The carousel's arrows' plate (#99): on 1366×650, 1440×900 and 1920×1080 in hours and days in the slider's row 12 px from the right, level with the row and at least 8 px from it, its buttons 40 × 40, the row still 560 px, and apart from the phrase, the row and the cards in view; on 720×500 the row narrower than 560 px, about 500, its cell about 59 px (a day's about 80), still 8 px from the plate; on 719×500 and 720×499 the plate over the cards 12 px from the left and 8 px over them, apart from the phrase, the slider and the first card; and on each touch window no plate, though the cards scroll.
- `e2e/modals.spec.ts`, the five modals on each wide window: 480 px in the middle, the top 10 % down and no higher than 12 px, in the window; «Гражданство», «Мои визы» and the list of all 640 px high or down to 12 px over the bottom, with no filter and a narrow one; «Откуда едем» and «Что важно знать» as high as what they hold, «Откуда едем» growing down; the scrim beside the modal, Esc and «Закрыть» close it.
- `e2e/start.spec.ts`: «Город», «На карте» and «Где я» on one line, on the card, at 390×844 and 1440×900 in either theme; the pin mode from the keyboard: the canvas, named «Карта», focused once it is on and described by the hint, an arrow moving the map's centre, Enter doing nothing, Tab to «Отмена» and «Старт здесь», Esc as «Отмена», and the focus back on the origin's word after «Отмена», Esc and «Старт здесь»; and the hint with a mouse and with touch. `e2e/cities.spec.ts` checks the no-break space of the population, by its text content, since `toHaveText` and `innerText` with `\s+` take it for a space. The row's drags and tab are in `e2e/days.spec.ts` on 1440×900.
- `e2e/wide-cities.spec.ts`, how a city is chosen on the wide screen (#104), on 1440×900 with a mouse and 768×1024 with touch: scrolling the row chooses nothing and leaves the map; a card the edge cuts (`cutCard`), clicked or tapped (`pressCard`, on the part in view), chooses its city, the map goes to it, and the row moves by just the cut, the card whole at the right edge and still under the pointer; pressed again once the map was dragged, it brings the map back at its zoom; a city tapped on the map or picked in the list whose card is out of view brings it whole to the nearer edge (`wholeAt`), one in view leaves the row; Tab shows the focused card whole and chooses nothing. The map going to a city is read as the city within 80 px of the map's middle (`fromTheMapMiddle`). On 1440×900 → 390×844 the chosen card comes to the left edge, and with no city chosen or one with no card nothing is chosen; the map stays in all three. On those two and 390×844 the keyboard's focus on a card and the chosen card's mark differ by their computed outlines. The narrow screen's carousel stays in `e2e/cities.spec.ts`, a short swipe that stops at the chosen card again bringing the map back to it among its checks.
- `e2e/wide-camera.spec.ts`, the camera on every wide window (#108), reading the panels' boxes and where the map projects the ring's frame and the city (`whereOnScreen`): with the longest phrase and the sources in its plate the new ring whole from 12 px under the plate to 12 px over the slider's row and 12 px from the edges, and the map's centre and zoom the same once the sources leave; a city chosen by a click on its card in the middle of that area; a ring that comes under the open «Мои визы» whole in it once the modal closes. The narrow screen's camera stays in `e2e/cities.spec.ts` and `e2e/ring.spec.ts`.
- `e2e/carousel-arrows.spec.ts`, what the arrows do: on 1440×900 a step forward and back by the part in view (forward the card the right edge cut before the step whole at the left edge), the grey arrow at either end with the plate unchanged, the buttons out of Tab, the wheel by its turn both ways, ← → moving the focus with the row following it, none of it choosing a city, and the plate gone with no origin, in the pin mode and with one city; on 719×500 a step, a turn of the wheel and → each a card, the stop choosing the city at the left edge, and the plate gone with no origin and in the pin mode; on 768×1024 with touch the wheel and → doing nothing of theirs. It and `wide-cities.spec.ts` open the same ring (`openTheCarousel` of `e2e/screen.ts`, the answer `carouselRing` of `e2e/fixtures.ts`): 12 cards of points of interest and «и ещё», a row that scrolls on every window.
- `e2e/hover.spec.ts`, hover and the map's cursor (#105): a city card's background (its computed colour and image) under the mouse differs from the one with the mouse away on 1440×900 and 390×844 with a mouse, and is the same on 390×844 and 768×1024 with touch; on 1440×900 a failure's first card keeps its background under the mouse while its «Повторить» answers; and the cursor the map's canvas shows is `pointer` over a city where the map projects it, `grab` over the origin with no city near, and `grab` over the city in the pin mode.
