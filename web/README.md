# web

The client: the one screen of the site ([spec #20](../docs/specs/20.md) «Экран», «Состояние, ссылка и память браузера»), variant C of the prototype (branch `claude/you-mshpw6`, `prototypes/screen/`, `#c`), whose layout and copy it ports. Vite, React, TanStack Router, Query and Virtual, MapLibre GL. `vite build` writes `dist/`, which the Worker `roadtrip` serves as its static assets (`worker/wrangler.jsonc`, `"assets"`) before its own routes.

## Where things are

One line per file, so that two changes of the screen seldom touch the same line.

- `src/main.tsx`:
  - PostHog on the production host (`src/analytics/`, «Analytics» below). posthog-js and the map view with MapLibre are chunks of their own, which the first paint does not wait for: the screen's chunk is 0.42 MB, 132 KB in gzip.
  - The opening state (`src/state/startup.ts`: the link, else the saved state, else the defaults), then the router (its context says where the opening state came from) and the Query client.
- `src/router.tsx`: one route `/` with hash history; `validateSearch`, `parseSearch` and `stringifySearch` from `src/state/search.ts` keep the link literal: `/#/?p=44.81760,20.45730&h=3&cit=RUS,SRB&visas=GEO,SCHENGEN`, or `d=2` in place of `h` for a ring of days.
- `src/state/`: the state in the link and its defaults.
  - `search.ts`: the origin, the slider's cell (`hours` of the state, its ring's upper bound: N, or `HOURS_PER_DAY`·D for day D; `h=N` or `d=D` in the link, never both: both, or either off the scale, give 1 h), the citizenships (Sherpa's passports sorted, `RUS` by default) and the held visas (`rules`' `HELD_VISAS` sorted, none by default and left out of the link then); anything else in `cit` or `visas` takes the default.
  - `point.ts`: the origin as a point to 5 decimals.
  - `startup.ts`: the saved snapshot.
  - `use-screen-state.ts`: the changes, each a `navigate` with `replace: true` plus the snapshot, and how the origin was set, which the link does not keep (`start`, `setStart`).
  - `settings.ts`: what besides the origin and N a ring is asked for, the citizenships and the held visas, which `useScreenState` gives as `settings`, one object while they stay.
- `src/ring/`:
  - `fetch-ring.ts`: the one place a ring request starts and ends.
  - `use-ring.ts`: the rings in TanStack Query (infinite `staleTime` and `gcTime`, no retries, the previous ring of the same origin as `placeholderData`) and the cells in memory, hours and days, which the slider marks; a ring is asked for with `from` and `to`, the bounds of its cell (`cellRing` of `worker/api`).
    - A `visa_rules_missing` is no ring: once the screen asks for something else it is removed from memory, so its hours get no mark and asking for it again asks the Worker (its card says «Попробуйте позже»).
- `src/start/`: the origin.
  - `geolocation.ts`: the browser's position and why it failed.
  - `start.ts`: `Start`, the origin and how it was set (`StartMethod` `geolocation`, `search`, `pin`, `link` or `saved`, with the city of the search); `startLabel`, the phrase's label: the city of the search, «на карте» for the pin, else the origin's city, «на карте» outside the limits of every city and until the cities file comes or if it cannot.
  - `start-sheet.tsx`: «Откуда едем»: the search, «Указать на карте», «Где я» and the note when the browser gave no position.
- `src/map/`:
  - `map-view.tsx`: the map view: the pin; in the pin mode the canvas has the focus and the pin panel's hint describes it (`aria-describedby`, #107); and the map announced to the tests.
  - `use-map.ts`: the MapLibre map, made once, its canvas named «Карта» to a screen reader (`Map.Title` of its `locale`); the overlay kept in step with the screen; a tap on a city, and the cursor over one (#105): over a city's dot or name, by the tap's own hit test and radius (`cityAt`, `TAP_RADIUS` 16 px), a pointer, with a mouse or not; elsewhere the map's `grab`, `grabbing` while it is dragged, and `grab` everywhere in the pin mode, where a tap picks nothing. The pointer is MapLibre's class `maplibregl-track-pointer` on the canvas's container, whose stylesheet keeps `grabbing` over it in a drag; the city's dot and name stay as they are.
  - `camera.ts`: where the map looks: over Europe, a new origin, a new ring whole, and `MapHandle`, the centre for the pin and a city to go to.
    - The room it leaves for the panels: on the narrow screen the fixed `MAP_PADDING`; on the wide one the free area (#108), which the screen gives as `freeArea` and the camera reads only when it moves, for a new ring or a city chosen. A new ring shows whole in it, a chosen city in its middle; a new origin comes to the middle of the screen, under the pin, on either. What changes later, the data's sources leaving the plate, the window's size, a modal opening or closing, moves nothing, and a ring that comes under an open modal is shown as it will be once the modal closes, since a modal moves no panel. There is no case of its own for little room: at 720 × 500 with the longest phrase and the sources in its plate the ring gets 83 px of height (141 px with the shortest).
  - `overlay.ts`: the sources and layers over the basemap, in the order the screen draws them: the hatching of closed countries, the ring, the ring's cities, the origin.
  - `basemap.ts`: the basemap's style.
- `src/cities/`: the cities in the ring (#32).
  - `cities.ts`: fetches the cities file (`/data/cities.json`, data's `build-cities` with the Russian names of `russian-names`, format `data/cities-file`) once into TanStack Query (`useCitiesFile`); `biggestFirst`, the order of every list of cities.
  - `in-ring.ts`: picks the ring's cities (`citiesInRing(cities, { ring, access, origin })`: inside the MultiPolygon, by a band index of its edges, in a zone `access` opens, never the origin's city, biggest first).
  - `city-card.tsx`: the card of a city (its name and right after it, on its line, its country's flag, named in `title` and `aria-label`, outside the heading; under them «Население: 1,7 млн»; «Маршрут»), «и ещё K / Все города →», and `CityRowText`, a city in a row of the search and of the list of all: its name with the flag, and under them the population. Neither shows the local name, the entry mode or Russian plates (#73).
  - `cities-sheet.tsx`: the list of all, TanStack Virtual, rows of a fixed height.
  - `copy.ts`: population, «Население: 1,7 млн», a no-break space before «млн» and «тыс.» so that «697 тыс.» never breaks (#101), the route in Google Maps.
  - `carousel.ts`: the carousel's current card when it stops, the one at its left edge, and scrolling a card there, smoothly or at once (`alignCard`); on the wide screen showing a card whole by its nearer edge (`showCard`), and the card that holds the focus (`showFocusedCard`); where the arrows' step takes the row, the part in view on the wide screen (`pageTarget`) and a card on the narrow one (`cardTarget`), and the card next to the focused one (`neighbourToFocus`); and a stop that chooses nothing, the one of the scroll a change of the layout makes (`skipLayoutStop`).
  - `search.ts`: `searchCities(cities, text)`, up to 8 by the start of the Russian or local name, never by a later word, case and ё aside, a hyphen as a space, bigger cities higher; `searchable`, which the sheets' filters use too.
  - `nearest.ts`: `nearestCity(cities, { lat, lon })`, by great-circle distance, no limit; its `zone` is its country.
    - `originCity`: the origin's city of GLOSSARY «Город точки старта», the city whose limits hold the origin, the nearest of several, none outside them all, by `cityAround` of `data/cities-file`, the builder's limits.
  - The origin's city (#45) is no city of the ring at any N: no card, no label on the map, no row of the list of all, and the first card, `cities_count` and `countries_count` leave it out.
    - The phrase names it for geolocation, a link and the saved state, and says «на карте» outside the limits of every city, as for the pin; `start_city` of the events stays the nearest city. The parts of a city (Zemun in Belgrade) are not in the cities file at all (`data/AGENTS.md`, «Cities file»).
- `index.html`: the page's head (#70): the name Роадтрип and the description, the icons, the manifest, `theme-color` of each scheme, and Open Graph and Twitter for a link's preview (below, «Mark»).
- `public/basemap/`: Protomaps' fonts and sprites, served as they are (below, «Basemap»).
- `public/favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `og.png`: the mark and what is made of it, all written by `tools/icons.ts`; `manifest.webmanifest` by hand (below, «Mark»).
- `src/screen/`:
  - `screen.tsx`: lays the screen out: `screen-map.tsx` (the map view, a chunk of its own, and the cities it shows), `screen-phrase.tsx` and the slider in one column (`.c-top`, below; on the wide screen the slider is a row over the carousel), `carousel.tsx` (the first card, the city cards, «и ещё») under the slider or `pin-bar.tsx` in the pin mode, and `screen-sheets.tsx` (the sheet open).
  - `use-screen.ts`: the screen's state and what changes it (`useScreen`, the `ScreenModel` the parts take): the link's state, then the hooks below, and the sheet open.
  - `use-shown-ring.ts`: the ring asked for, the answer shown, the ring's cities, the hours in memory, the events.
  - `use-start-methods.ts`: geolocation, at «Где я» and on a load without an origin; the search; the pin mode, which gives the focus back to the origin's word in the phrase (`startSlot`) however it ends.
  - `pin-bar.tsx`: the pin mode's panel (#107): the hint, with a mouse «Двигайте карту мышью или стрелками, …» (`useMedia('mouse')`), «Отмена» and «Старт здесь». It comes right after the map in the DOM, so Tab goes canvas → «Отмена» → «Старт здесь»; Esc is «Отмена» but in a sheet; Enter on the canvas does nothing.
  - `carousel-arrows.tsx`: the carousel with a mouse (#99, #113), at any width and only with one (`useMedia('mouse')`; without a mouse the wheel and the keys stay the browser's):
    - The plate ‹ › (`CarouselArrows`, `data-testid="carousel-arrows"`), whose buttons «Листать карточки назад» and «Листать карточки вперёд» are out of the order of Tab and leave the focus where it was. On the wide screen a step is the part of the row in view: forward the first card the right edge cuts comes to the left one, back the cards before the first whole one fill the view up to the right edge; on the narrow one a card. At the row's end that way an arrow is grey and does nothing (`disabled`); where the row does not scroll (all its cards fit, or the first card is all it holds) there is no plate, nor in the pin mode, which has no carousel. The row's ends are read when the carousel is seen first and at each change of its size, its cards and its scroll.
    - The vertical wheel over the row scrolls it, smoothly by its turn on the wide screen, a card a turn on the narrow one; Shift with the wheel, a sideways swipe and a pinch stay the browser's. Steps and turns add up: the next starts where the last aims while the row is on its way there.
    - ← and → on a card move the focus to the next card that holds a button or a link (not the ring's first card), and the row follows: on the wide screen `onCarouselFocus` shows it whole, on the narrow one it comes to the left edge.
    - They only scroll `chosen.carousel`: on the wide screen nothing is chosen, on the narrow one the stop chooses, as after a finger's swipe.
  - `use-chosen-city.ts`: the chosen city: a card tapped (the name's button, whose area covers the card but «Маршрут»), a city tapped on the map or picked in the list, and on the narrow screen the carousel's current card, the one at its left edge; a new ring chooses none. The rule is the window's size (`useMedia('wide')`), not the pointer's (#104):
    - On the narrow screen any stop of the carousel chooses the current card's city, and a card tapped, a city tapped or picked is scrolled there for its stop to choose it (`choosingOnNarrow`). A stop at the city already chosen chooses it again, and brings back to it the map led away.
    - On the wide screen the row only scrolls, whatever scrolls it (a finger, the wheel, Tab, a script), and its stop chooses nothing. A card pressed (a click, a tap, Enter or Space on its name's button), a city tapped on the map or picked in the list is chosen at once and the map goes to it, and the row moves only to show its card whole, by the nearer edge, or not at all when it is whole in view (`choosingOnWide`). A city with no card is chosen with no card marked; tapped on the map, it opens the list of all, as on the narrow screen. The focus come into a card shows it whole (`onCarouselFocus`), its city not chosen.
    - A window that turns narrow puts the chosen city's card at the left edge at once and leaves the map; with none chosen, or a city with no card, the row stays where it was. The narrow layout snaps the row anew, and that stop chooses nothing (`skipLayoutStop` of `carousel.ts`): the city chosen, or none, stays, and the map. A window that turns wide changes nothing.
  - `first-card-state.ts`: the first card's state.
  - `phrase.tsx`: the phrase, with `VisaRulesDate`, «визовые правила от <дата>».
  - `attribution.tsx`: the map's attribution: `DataSources`, the line of «Что важно знать», and `SourcesOnOpen`, the same line under the phrase for 5 s once the screen opens (`useSourcesOnOpen`, which `screen.tsx` holds, so a window crossing the threshold does not show it again), as OpenStreetMap's attribution guidelines want it seen before it folds into ⓘ.
  - `clock.ts`: the time to the minute, for the rules' date and today's rows of «Мои визы».
  - `slider.tsx`: the hours 1…`MAX_HOURS`, then the days 2…`MAX_DAYS` (`SLIDER_CELLS` of `worker/api`, #83), one unit at a time: in hours a column of a cell an hour, 1 h at the bottom, each the ring from N − 1 to N h, and the tab «дни ↑» over it; in days a large cell a day, 2 at the bottom, each the ring from `HOURS_PER_DAY`·(D − 1) to `HOURS_PER_DAY`·D h, and «часы ↓» under it. The tab is as wide as the panel, and a tap 8 px past it takes it too. The panel and the tab are opaque. The chosen cell is blue and reads «6–7», or «2–3» over «дня», the others N or D, those of the rings in memory light green and `data-cached`; a drag chooses the cell under the finger, or the tab past the column's end, and asks for its ring when let go, a tap chooses one, a key steps through hours and days alike. The column turns to the other unit only once that is committed, never under the finger. On the wide screen (#97) the same slider, its state, cells, keys and commit, is a row: the cells 1…`MAX_HOURS` or 2…`MAX_DAYS` from the left, «дни →» right of the hours and «← часы» left of the days, a drag along the row and past its end onto the tab as along the column, and `aria-orientation` horizontal (`useMedia('wide')`, below). Here the screen departs from the prototype, whose track and thumb it had; the days are the prototype Z2F of #77 (branch `claude/vibrant-mendel-47wxtt`, commit 25da3de).

On the narrow screen it is a sheet from the bottom, on the wide one a modal in the middle (`styles.css`, below); `steady`, for «Гражданство», «Мои визы» and the list of all, keeps the modal one height whatever the filter.

- `src/media.json`: the screen's media queries by name, each written only here (#96). `wide`, the **wide screen**, `(min-width: 720px) and (min-height: 500px)`: the window's size alone, never the device, the user agent or a touch screen; the rest is the **narrow screen**, a phone on its side too. `mouse`, `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, at any width. `dark`, the dark scheme, and `reduced-motion`, a person who reduces motion.
  - The CSS writes `@media (--wide)`, `@media (--dark)` and so on, custom media as Media Queries 5 has them, and the build puts the query of that name in their place (`customMedia` in `vite.config.ts`). A name not in the file fails the build, and so does one under `not`, after `or` or in a list: the CSS writes the narrow screen first and the wide one over it. Stylelint takes no query written out in `@media` (`media-feature-name-allowed-list`, empty, in `stylelint.config.mjs`): only these names.
  - The JS reads the file through `src/media.ts`: what is read once by `matchesMedia(name)`, the basemap's scheme (`map/basemap.ts`) and whether the stripes run (`map/overlay.ts`); a component by `useMedia('wide')` or `useMedia('mouse')` (`src/use-media.ts`, which renders again when the answer changes), only where the behaviour or the DOM changes with the layout: the slider's drag and orientation, the pin mode's hint, how a city is chosen (`use-chosen-city.ts`), where `screen.tsx` puts the data's sources (in the phrase's plate on the wide screen, under the phrase keeping their place on the narrow one) and the camera's free area (`.c-free`, below), and the carousel's arrows, wheel and keys (`carousel-arrows.tsx`); the look is the CSS's. A window that crosses the threshold remounts nothing: the CSS changes the look, and what is open, typed, scrolled or chosen stays.
- `src/styles.css`: the prototype's CSS for variant C, its tokens on `:root` with the dark scheme; the slider's `.sl*`, its panel `.c-vs`, the column `.c-top` and the carousel's layout are ours.
  - The sheets (#98): on the narrow screen `.sheet` comes up from the bottom, the whole width; on the wide one it is a modal in the middle, 480 px or the window less `--edge` on each side, its top 10 % down the window and no higher than `--edge-top`, growing down to `--edge-bottom` over the bottom at most, its corners `--radius`. A steady one is 640 px high or down to that limit and its body scrolls; the others are as high as what they hold. The scrim is the same, 35 %.
  - The tokens of the layout (#85): every floating panel stands `--edge` (12 px) from the screen's nearest edges past the safe area (`--edge-top` …), `--between` (8 px) from the panel next to it; the floating panels and plates have corners of `--radius` (18 px), what is nested in them, the slider's cells and the buttons, `--radius-inner` (12 px).   - `.c-top` holds the phrase, the data's sources (`SourcesOnOpen`, `src/screen/attribution.tsx`) and the slider, from the top edge down to the bottom one, over the carousel (#47, #85). The sources are as wide as the phrase and show for 5 s once the screen opens; then they hide in their place, which stays, so the slider under them never moves and they never cover it (#77). The slider is up to 450 px high at the bottom of the column; a longer phrase moves its top down, 8 px under the sources' place at the closest, and never makes it lower than 120 px.
  - At any width the keyboard's focus (`:focus-visible`) is an outline of `--fg` 3 px out, never blue, and the chosen city's card (`.c-card.chosen`) is marked with 2 px of `--ring` close to it (#104): a card can have both. A city card's focus is on its tap's `::after`, which covers the card.
  - Hover (#105), only with a mouse (`@media (--mouse)`, at the end of the file) and at any width: what a click does something on answers the mouse over it with its colour, and only that. Buttons (`.btn`, «Закрыть» `.ico-btn`, ⓘ `.c-info`, the arrows' enabled buttons), the slider's cell and tab, the sheets' rows (`.pick-item`, `.row`) and the cards one can click (`.c-card.clickable`: a city card, its whole tap area, and «и ещё K») get a veil of `--hover` over whatever ground they have, a little darker in either theme; the phrase's words but the hours (`.tok:not(.static)`) and «Маршрут» turn their dotted underline solid. ## For the next tickets

- **Map layers.** `src/map/overlay.ts` says where each goes. Add sources and layers in `addOverlay`, and a `show…` function the map view calls with props.
- **The hatching.** The layer `closed-hatch` fills the zone outlines (source `zones`, fetched from `/data/zones.json`, data's `zoneOutlines`) whose code `access` of the answer shown says `closed`, by a filter (`showClosed`); without an answer with `access`, nothing. The outlines are the dataset's: `pnpm --filter data build-graph <extract> <out.graph> --outlines web/public/data/zones.json` puts them where `vite dev` and `vite build` take them (gitignored); the deploy puts the dataset's there before the build (`worker/README.md`, «Deploy»). Without the file the map has no hatching and nothing else changes.
- **The cities file** goes there too: `pnpm --filter data build-cities <graph> web/public/data/cities.json --countries RS,HU,…` for a region (`data/AGENTS.md`), then `pnpm --filter data russian-names` for the table's Russian names, as the deploy puts them in the dataset's. Without it the ring's card says «В кольце …» with no count and the carousel has no cities.
- **The phrase.** `ScreenPhrase` (`src/screen/screen-phrase.tsx`) fills it in. `Phrase` takes `settings` (after «в пути.»: «Гражданство [РФ], мои визы: [нет]», each a `PhraseToken` that opens its sheet), `footer` (`VisaRulesDate`, and on the wide screen the data's sources under it) and `startLabel` (`startLabel()` of `src/start/start.ts`). Sheets are `Sheet`s (`src/screen/sheets.tsx`).
- **The cities (#33, #34).** `useCitiesFile()` has the cities file (`City`: id, Russian name or else local, the local name when it differs, place, population, zone), and `use-shown-ring.ts` the ring's but the origin's city (`shownCities`, undefined while the ring is an old one or the file has not come): the search (`searchCities`) and the nearest city (`nearestCity`) take the same list, #34 counts `cities_count` and `countries_count` from it as the first card does. The carousel's other points of interest would go where `CityCard` does. The local name is not shown, only searched: «Budapest» finds Будапешт.
- **Panels over the map.** A new one joins `e2e/layout.spec.ts`, which checks that the panels are apart, on the screen and 12 px from its edges on five phones, and the wide screen's checks (`e2e/wide-layout.spec.ts`, `e2e/README.md`) on the windows of `e2e/windows.ts`.
- **Media queries.** CSS takes `@media (--wide)`, `@media (--mouse)` and the other names of `src/media.json`, JS `matchesMedia` or `useMedia` of them (above); never a width, a pointer or a preference of their own.
- **Scrolling the carousel.** Whatever scrolls it (the arrows, the wheel and the keys of `carousel-arrows.tsx`, a finger, Tab) scrolls `chosen.carousel` and needs nothing else: its stop chooses the current card on the narrow screen and nothing on the wide one (`use-chosen-city.ts`). A focus moved to another card shows that card whole on the wide screen (`onCarouselFocus`).
- **Analytics.** A new event goes in `src/analytics/events.ts` (its properties) and is sent with `send` of `src/analytics/posthog.ts`, or through `ScreenAnalytics` when it needs the place (below, «Analytics»).

## Analytics

`src/analytics/` ([spec #20](../docs/specs/20.md) «Аналитика и ошибки», «Браузер»; decisions #16–#18):

- `posthog.ts` starts PostHog in `main.tsx` before anything else, and only on the production host `roadtrip.kamyshev.me`: anywhere else (`vite dev`, `vite preview`, a copy) nothing is loaded and `send` does nothing. On it, `posthog-client.ts`, posthog-js itself, comes as a chunk of its own, imported at once but not waited for by the first paint; until it is up, `send` keeps the events with the time they happened, the errors the page throws are kept and handed to it, and `fetchRing` waits for it (`analyticsStarted`), so the first ring request carries the tracing headers too. The settings are the spec's: `defaults: '2026-08-30'` (a page view only when the path changes, never for the link's `#…`, and `#…` cut from every URL in the events), `capture_exceptions`, no session recording, the proxy `https://r-rt.kamyshev.me` as `api_host`, `ui_host`, and `tracing_headers` for the production host, so the ring requests carry `X-POSTHOG-DISTINCT-ID` and `X-POSTHOG-SESSION-ID` for the Worker's events and errors. The SDK's tracing headers and exception autocapture are bundled rather than loaded from the proxy after `init`, so the first ring request of a load carries the ids and an error while the page loads is caught.
- `events.ts`: the screen's own events and their properties, as decision #18 has them: `start set`, `geolocation failed`, `ring result`, `link opened`. Never the origin: a place is the nearest city of the cities file (`start_city`, `start_city_id`) and its country (`start_country`).
- `screen-analytics.ts` says when each goes; `use-screen-analytics.ts` feeds it from the screen (`src/screen/use-shown-ring.ts`). `link opened` goes from `main.tsx`, `geolocation failed` from the screen's failed `locate()` (`src/screen/use-start-methods.ts`).
  - `start set` and `ring result` wait for the cities file for their place (and a ring for its `cities_count` and `countries_count`, counted as the first card does), and go with the time they happened; without the file they go without them.
  - `ring result` is one for each ring asked for, its `hours` the ring's upper bound (`HOURS_PER_DAY`·D for day D, #83): the load, the slider let go (it changes N only then, so moving it sends nothing), other settings, another origin, «Повторить». A ring in memory is `memory` with `wait_ms` 0; a request ends with its answer (`source` the Worker's), its failure (`too_many_requests`, `error`), or `abandoned` when the screen asks for something else first, at once, or when the page goes away (`pagehide`), by `sendBeacon`, as everything still waiting.
- The map's container has `ph-no-deadclick` and `ph-no-rageclick`: a tap on the map changes only the canvas, and PostHog's dead clicks (once the project turns them on) took every tap for one.

### Source maps

`@posthog/rollup-plugin` in `vite.config.ts` uploads the build's source maps to PostHog, so that the stack of an error in the browser reads as the source. It runs only when the build has two variables, which Workers Builds gives the build of the Worker `roadtrip` (its trigger's build variables, not the Worker's vars):

- `POSTHOG_PERSONAL_API_KEY`: a secret, the Owner's personal key (`phx_…`) with the scope «error tracking: write». Never in the repository.
- `POSTHOG_PROJECT_ID`: `718601`, not secret.

With both, the plugin writes a chunk id into each JS file, uploads the maps under the release `roadtrip-web` of the commit's git SHA (`WORKERS_CI_COMMIT_SHA`, else `git rev-parse HEAD`) and removes the `.map` files from `dist/`: the deploy must ship exactly that `dist/`. A key PostHog refuses fails the build. Without them, as locally, in CI and in the screen tests, the plugin is left out: the build is as before and uploads nothing. The plugin runs `posthog-cli`, which downloads its binary from `releases.posthog.com` on first use (pnpm runs no install script of `@posthog/cli`).

## Older browsers

The screen runs where MapLibre 6 runs: Safari 15.4, Chrome 93, Firefox 92 and newer (`browserslist` in `package.json`, which `vite.config.ts` reads and Stylelint checks the CSS against; MapLibre's worker calls `.at()` and `Object.hasOwn`). The build lowers the syntax to them, and `older-browsers.ts` gives them what the client calls and they lack: `@vitejs/plugin-legacy` makes the polyfills (core-js, by what the bundle uses) a chunk of their own, and the page loads its entry through a small loader that takes that chunk first only in a browser without `toSorted`, `Object.groupBy` or `Promise.withResolvers`. So the client may call what `tsconfig.json`'s lib (es2024) has, and a newer browser downloads no polyfill: the first paint's budget is its (`bundle-budget.ts`). `e2e/older-browsers.spec.ts` checks both: Chromium without those methods loads the polyfills and shows the screen, and as it is asks for none.

## Basemap

`src/map/basemap.ts` ([spec #20](../docs/specs/20.md) «Экран» → «Карта»): the style of `@protomaps/basemaps` over the vector tiles of `GET /tiles/{z}/{x}/{y}`, which the Worker serves from the world's PMTiles archive (`worker/README.md`). MapLibre's attribution control is off: the screen credits the data itself (`src/screen/attribution.tsx`), as the licences want it (`docs/attribution.md`).

- The flavor is `light`, or `dark` when the page is in the dark scheme, read once when the map is made.
- Labels: Protomaps' `lang: 'ru'` writes the Russian name, else the English one, with the local name on a second line. Every label that shows a name gets `coalesce(name:ru, name)` instead: Russian, else local, on one line, countries too. The regions keep their abbreviation below z6; house numbers and road shields keep their text.
- Fonts and sprites are ours, in `public/basemap/`, from `protomaps/basemaps-assets` at `028c18f`: the v4 sprites of `light` and `dark`, and of the three fontstacks the style uses (`Noto Sans Regular`, `Medium`, `Italic`) only the glyph ranges of Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Armenian, Georgian and punctuation: `0-255`, `256-511`, `512-767`, `768-1023`, `1024-1279`, `1280-1535`, `4096-4351`, `8192-8447` (2.2 MB; every range is 13.5 MB). MapLibre draws a character of any other range with the browser's own font, and the scripts that need shaping (Devanagari, Thai and the like) and Chinese, Japanese and Korean always. `fonts/OFL.txt` is the fonts' licence; the sprites derive from the MIT-licensed `tangrams/icons`.
- Another range or flavor: copy it from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/protomaps/basemaps-assets/028c18f/fonts/<fontstack>/<range>.pbf` (or `sprites/v4/<flavor>{,@2x}.{json,png}`) into the same path under `public/basemap/`.

## Tests

The screen's seam ([spec #20](../docs/specs/20.md) «Решения по тестам», 3) is Playwright on the built client: `e2e/*.spec.ts`.

**The doubles** (`e2e/fixtures.ts`). `/api/ring` is a scripted double that records each request (its headers too) and answers or holds it (`RingApi`, `e2e/ring-api.ts`); `visaRules()` adds `access` and `visaRulesLoadedAt` to an answer. The tiles of `/tiles/` are empty vector tiles, `/data/zones.json` gets a few squares around Belgrade (`ZONE_OUTLINES`), `/data/cities.json` real cities around Belgrade (`CITIES`, or a test's own through `serveCities`). Every other host is cut off in every test (the `network` fixture, which records both): a link to another site opens Chromium's error page, and the test reads the URL it asked for in `network.blocked`. Under the routes, every test's context goes through a proxy where nothing listens (`e2e/offline.ts`), so a request no route answers fails in the browser: the beacons of a page already gone, which Playwright's routes miss, reach neither PostHog nor its proxy (`analytics.spec.ts` checks it). Geolocation comes from the context's permissions.

**Time** comes from `page.clock`. A test that measures time or waits for it (the 5 s of «досчитываем», a `wait_ms`, the map's easing, PostHog's dead clicks) moves the page's clock, and none waits on real time, so they hold on a busy machine. A test holds the clock through `holdTheClock` of `e2e/screen.ts` (below), which Oxlint's `roadtrip/clock-through-screen` checks: it takes no `clock.install`, `setFixedTime` or `setSystemTime` in `e2e/` outside `screen.ts`, each of which installs a running clock when none is. `runFor`, `fastForward`, `pauseAt` and `resume` install one too when none is, but the rule leaves them alone, since it cannot tell whether a clock is held where they are called: they only move the clock after `holdTheClock`, and the review checks that. The few that need a running clock disable it on that line with the reason: a date for the visa rules (`held-visas.spec.ts`, `visa.spec.ts`), the carousel and the map of `cities.spec.ts`, PostHog's dead clicks. A step that scrolls the carousel and then moves the clock first waits for the scroll's `scrollend`: the browser sends `scroll` with a later frame, and the carousel's stop counts its 140 ms from that event (`openTheList` in `e2e/cities.spec.ts`, #115).

**Reading the screen.** The tests read it by role, text and `data-testid`, never by a class or tag of its styles (`playwright/no-raw-locators`, which allows only the state attributes listed in `.oxlintrc.json`). They read the map by its sources, layers and the hatching's filter, never by pixels (the icons of `head.spec.ts` aside, below, «Mark»), through the MapLibre map the client announces with the event `roadtrip:map` (`MAP_EVENT` in `src/map/map-view.tsx`), which an init script of the fixtures keeps as `window.mapForTests` (`e2e/screen.ts`): the deployed build, the one tested, puts nothing on `window`. A tap on a city goes where the map projects it (`tapMap`).

**Shared helpers.** A locator or a reading of the screen that more than one spec needs (`phrase`, `slider`, `carousel`, `boxOf`, the panels' 12 px and 8 px `EDGE` and `BETWEEN`…) lives in `e2e/screen.ts`; the data they share, the places, the links to Belgrade's rings (built on `FROM_BELGRADE`, which is in `screen.ts` for `openTheCarousel` too, as are the rings of the phrase's links, `LAST_HOUR_RING` and `LAST_DAY_RING`, for `openHeld`), the longest and shortest phrases, `OPEN_AROUND_SERBIA` and `OLD_RULES`, in `e2e/fixtures.ts`. `pnpm lint` checks it (`lint/e2e-names.ts`): no two specs declare one top-level function or constant, nor a spec one a shared module of `e2e/` has, which fallow's dupes miss when the copy is short. One thing moves to the shared module; two things get names that tell them apart.

**What the layout's specs check**, on the phones (`e2e/layout.spec.ts`) and on the wide screen's windows (`e2e/windows.ts`, `e2e/wide-*.spec.ts`, `modals`, `start`, `carousel-arrows`, `hover`): `e2e/README.md`. A new check of the layout adds its line there.

PostHog's tests (`e2e/analytics.spec.ts`) open the same build at `https://roadtrip.kamyshev.me` (`openInProduction`): the fixtures answer that host from the preview server, so the client runs PostHog there as in production, and nothing in it knows of tests. The `posthog` fixture is PostHog behind its proxy (`PostHogDouble`, `e2e/posthog-double.ts`): it records the events the page sends, the beacons marked, answers the remote config with the project's own (`captureDeadClicks` aside) and serves the SDK's lazy scripts from the installed `posthog-js`; the page shows the SDK a person's phone, not an automated browser, whose events it drops. Leaving the page is its `pagehide` (`leave`): Playwright does not see the beacons of a page that is really gone.

PostHog takes any change of the page in the seconds after a click for its answer, so a change of the screen on a timer turns the dead click of `analytics.spec.ts` into none: its test lets the page's clock run past every such timer (the data's sources under the phrase, 5 s) before it taps, and a new timer of the screen joins them there.

The tests wait for the screen's state, not its copy: the first card's `data-state` names its state (`FirstCardState` of `src/screen/first-card.tsx`: `ring`, `no_cities`, `calculating`, `empty`, `error`…), and `expectRingCard` (`e2e/screen.ts`) waits for a ring's answer by it and the slider. A new load of the page, as after closing the tab, is `reopen` (`e2e/screen.ts`): it first waits until the open page has asked, since it was loaded, for the ring its link names: that request goes after the link and the phrase change. Only a test about a text reads the text (`CODING_STANDARDS.md`, «Tests»). A test that holds the page's clock (`holdTheClock`, a minute after `CLOCK_START`; `openHeld` opens a link on it) runs it for the page to go on: MapLibre loads and flies by the page's frames, and TanStack Query tells of an answer on a timer, so a file or an answer that comes once the clock is held shows only as the clock runs. `runUntil` runs it a frame at a time until a state shows, `runUntilMapAtRest` until the map is up and at rest, and `settle` past every timer of the screen.

```bash
pnpm e2e                               # at the root: builds the client, serves it with `vite preview` on this checkout's port, runs Chromium
pnpm e2e ring.spec                     # one file (a regex on the path)
pnpm e2e ring.spec.ts:37               # the test at that line
pnpm e2e -g 'a key moves'              # the tests whose title matches
pnpm e2e --last-failed                 # the tests that failed in the last run
pnpm e2e --repeat-each=3 --workers=4   # a stress check: every test 3 times, 4 at once (by default half the CPUs)
E2E_PORT=4180 pnpm e2e                 # on a port of your own
pnpm --filter web dev                  # the dev server; it sends /api to the worker's `pnpm dev` on :8787 (worker/README.md)
```

- **Flags** go right after `pnpm e2e`, or after `pnpm --filter web exec playwright test`, which is the same. Not after `--`: pnpm hands the `--` on to Playwright, which then drops what follows without a word and runs every test.
- **The port** is each checkout's own, one of 20000–29999 from the path of its `web/` (`playwright.config.ts`), so checkouts on one machine, agents' worktrees, run their tests at once; `E2E_PORT` gives another. A server already there is never taken for this checkout's: the run fails and says so. Stop it, perhaps an earlier run of this checkout, or give another port.
- **A failure** is in the console in full, and left in files for a later command to read whole, rather than a rerun or the console through `tail` or `grep`, which cut it: `web/test-results/failures.md` (each failed test with its message, code and stack, then the errors outside the tests, as a port taken; `e2e/failures.ts`, the console's last line names it) and `web/test-results/<test>/error-context.md` (the error, the page's aria snapshot, the test's source). The next run empties `test-results/`. On GitHub Actions each failed part (below, «On CI») keeps its `test-results/` for 7 days as its artifact `e2e-results-<part>`: `gh run download <run id> -p 'e2e-results-*'` takes them all, each in its own folder.
- **A flaky test** is a race between a step and the page. Find the step with a probe first: a throwaway test in `e2e/probes/`, which git ignores and the lint hook skips, that records, at that step, what the doubles have seen (`ringApi.calls.length`, say), run with `--repeat-each=200`, and deleted once it has answered: a probe left there runs in every local `pnpm e2e` and `pnpm typecheck`. A stress run then shows the fix holds. A stress run alone may stay green here: a cloud session has 4 CPUs, more `--workers` than that add no load, and a race that fails once in 100 on the Owner's machine passed 500 runs in one, at `--workers=16` too.
- **The output** is Playwright's list, plus GitHub's annotations on GitHub Actions only (`GITHUB_ACTIONS`): `CI=1` changes nothing in it, and only forbids `test.only`.
- **On CI** (`.github/workflows/checks.yml`) the tests run in 4 parts at once, the jobs `e2e 1/4` to `e2e 4/4`, beside the job `checks` with every other check: each part builds the client and runs its share, `pnpm e2e --shard=<part>/4`, with the default 2 workers (4 a part ran slower, #136). Playwright splits the tests by their count, in the files' order, not by their time, so the parts with the costly `wide-*` files run longest. Each part takes Chromium's headless shell from the Actions cache, keyed by the installed `@playwright/test`'s version, and installs it only on a cache miss (a new version, a cache unused for 7 days, a branch's first run before `main` saved one), with no system packages: the runner's image has Chrome's, and the client brings its fonts.

`@playwright/test` is pinned to the Chromium of the environment (`PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH`).
